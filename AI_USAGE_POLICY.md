# AI Usage Policy

Welcome to the openshelves project! We are excited to have you contribute. 

Many of our developers and maintainers embrace Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools (such as GitHub Copilot, Gemini, ChatGPT, and Claude) as a highly productive part of their workflow. **We are not anti-AI, and we welcome the use of AI as an assistive tool.**

However, because AI-generated code can sometimes introduce subtle bugs, security flaws, or architectural mismatches, we have established the following guidelines to ensure our codebase remains safe, high-quality, and maintainable.

## 1. AI is a Tool, Not a Substitute for Understanding

**The human-in-the-loop must fully understand all code submitted.** 
The ability to generate code with AI does not automatically bring the understanding needed to judge whether that code is correct, safe, or architecturally appropriate for this project. 
- If you cannot explain what your changes do, how they work, and how they interact with the greater system without the aid of AI tools, please do not submit them.
- If you lack the familiarity with the codebase to identify what could go wrong, we encourage you to ask questions first! We love to help developers learn and grow.

## 2. Developer Responsibility

**You are fully responsible for the code you submit.** 
The use of AI tools does not diminish your responsibility as a contributor. You must:
- Thoroughly review, test, and verify any code generated by AI before submitting a Pull Request.
- Ensure the code meets our coding standards and quality guidelines.
- Be accountable for any bugs, security vulnerabilities, or performance issues introduced by your contribution.

## 3. Data Privacy and Security

Protecting user data and project secrets is paramount.
- **Do not share sensitive information.** Never input API keys, credentials, sensitive user data, personally identifiable information (PII), or private security vulnerabilities into public AI tools.
- Avoid uploading proprietary internal architecture details to public AI chatbots that may use your inputs for training.

## 4. Intellectual Property and Licensing

Our project respects intellectual property rights.
- **Do not blindly copy/paste large blocks of code** if the AI is known to output verbatim code from improperly licensed or copyrighted sources.
- Ensure that your contributions do not violate third-party copyright, patents, or trade secrets.

## 5. Transparency and Attribution

We encourage transparency in how contributions are made.
- If a significant portion of your contribution was heavily generated or structured by AI, please disclose the tool you used in your Pull Request description.
- Example: *"Note: The boilerplate for this feature was generated using [AI Tool Name] and subsequently reviewed, modified, and tested."*

## 6. Issues, Discussions, and Bots

- **Issues and discussions:** You may use AI to help draft issues or discussion posts, but please review and heavily edit the content before submission. AI often tends toward verbosity and noise—trim it, focus it, and ensure it reflects your actual intent.
- **Bots:** Fully automated AI bots or agents are not permitted to open PRs, create issues, or review code without prior approval from the maintainers.

## Remember: There are Humans Here

Please remember that openshelves is maintained by humans. Every discussion, issue, and pull request is read, reviewed, and validated by our human maintainers.

We ask that you respect the time of our reviewers by ensuring your submissions are thoughtful, tested, and well-understood. Low-effort submissions composed of unreviewed AI output place an unfair burden of validation and debugging on our maintainers, and may be politely closed.

Thank you for your understanding, and happy coding!
