## Overview

OpenShelves is a community-driven open-source project stewarded by **OpenShelves Labs**, operating under the GitHub organization [`openshelves-labs`](https://github.com/openshelves-labs).

"OpenShelves Labs" is the legal entity responsible for the project's long-term stewardship, licensing authority, and business decisions, as referenced in the [Contributor License Agreement](./CLA.md).

This document defines who the Core Team is, what authority they hold, and how decisions are made — providing the concrete definition of "Core Team" as used throughout the [CLA](./CLA.md).

---

## Roles

### OpenShelves Labs
The founding entity behind the project. Holds final authority over:
- License changes and dual-licensing
- Monetization and commercial policy
- Governance structure changes
- Official partnerships and legal matters

Currently represented by the founding members listed in the Core Team below.

### Core Team
Day-to-day maintainers of the project with merge rights and policy authority. The Core Team acts on behalf of OpenShelves Labs for all technical and contributor decisions.

### Maintainers
Trusted contributors with code review and merge rights on specific modules or areas of the codebase. Appointed by the Core Team. Maintainers do not hold the same licensing or policy authority as the Core Team.

### Contributors
Anyone who submits a pull request, files an issue, or participates in Discussions. All contributors must agree to the [CLA](./CLA.md) before their code is merged.

### Community
Anyone using, discussing, or sharing OpenShelves. Community members may participate in Discussions and open issues freely without a CLA.

## Decision Making

| Type of decision | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Bug fix, documentation PR | Any Maintainer |
| New feature, API change | Core Team consensus |
| License or business policy | OpenShelves Labs |
| Governance changes | OpenShelves Labs |
| Contributor disputes | Core Team (final) |

For non-urgent changes — including license changes, monetization decisions, or governance amendments — the Core Team aims to open a public Discussion at least **14 days** before a decision takes effect, giving the community time to provide input. In cases where immediate action is required, such as legal, security, or compliance matters, changes may take effect without advance notice.

**The Core Team welcomes open discussion on all decisions, but retains final authority.** Individual contributors do not hold veto rights over project-level decisions by virtue of having contributed code. This is consistent with the terms of the [CLA #9](./CLA.md#9-dispute-resolution-and-governance).

## Becoming a Maintainer

Active contributors who demonstrate consistent, high-quality involvement may be invited to become Maintainers by the Core Team. There is no formal application process — the Core Team reaches out directly.

---

## Joining the Core Team

Core Team membership is by invitation from existing Core Team members or OpenShelves Labs. It is granted to individuals who have shown sustained commitment to the project over time.

Additions and removals from the Core Team are reflected immediately in the table above and announced via a GitHub Discussion.
