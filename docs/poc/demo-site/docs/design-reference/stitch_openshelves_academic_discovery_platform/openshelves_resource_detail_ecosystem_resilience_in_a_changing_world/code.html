Ecosystem Resilience in a Changing World: Adaptive Dynamics and Biome Restoration

A Comprehensive Synthesis of Multi-Decadal Biome Perturbation and Regeneration Models

Lead Author: Dr. Marion Vance (Inst. for Terrestrial Ecology)
•
Co-Author: Dr. Julian Thorne
•
Editor: Prof. H. Lindqvist (Foreword)
apartment Central University Press • Published October 2023 • 3rd Revised Edition • Language: English

subject Scholarly Abstract

Synthesizing over thirty-five years of empiric field data from ninety-four circumpolar, temperate, and sub-tropical biome observation stations, this monograph investigates multi-decadal recovery thresholds under accelerated climatic shifts. Dr. Vance and collaborators formalize a modular mathematical framework reconciling non-linear feedback loops between soil microbiome biodiversity, hydrological retention intervals, and vascular plant recruitment. Designed for theoretical ecologists and restoration practitioners alike, the text offers unified predictive models that bridge micro-scale biogeochemical assays with macro-scale landscape resilience protocols.
